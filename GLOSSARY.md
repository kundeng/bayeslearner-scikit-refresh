# Glossary

> Canonical term definitions. Once a term is defined here, that definition is used
> everywhere in the book. Proposing a different meaning requires a steering-level
> decision (STEERING.md §6.4).

| Term | Definition | First Introduced |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| *bias-variance decomposition* | Decomposition of expected prediction error into squared bias, variance, and irreducible noise | Ch. 1 §2 |
| *cross-validation* | Estimation of expected loss by repeated train/test splitting of the training data | Ch. 1 §2 |
| *CASH problem* | Combined Algorithm Selection and Hyperparameter optimization; the mathematical formulation of AutoML | Ch. 1 §2 |
| *pipeline* | A sequence of transformers followed by an estimator, applied sequentially | Ch. 1 §3 |
| *regularization path* | The trajectory of estimated coefficients as the regularization parameter varies | Ch. 1 §5 |
| *data leakage* | Using information from the test set (or future observations) during training or preprocessing | Ch. 1 §11 |
| *stationary point* | A point $\mathbf{x}^*$ where $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^*) = \mathbf{0}$ | Ch. 1 §4 |
